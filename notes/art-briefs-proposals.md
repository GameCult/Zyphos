# Zyphos Art Briefs (PROPOSALS)

**Not canon.** These are prompt-only image briefs drafted for Deru, who owns the setting and decides whether to run any of them, on any machine. Nothing here edits the vault or has been rendered. Author: Weksa.

## How to read this

- Each brief is one finished caption, written in the style FLUX.2 [klein] prompting favours: one flowing paragraph of plain sentences, subject first, then setting, light, composition and medium, all stated positively (no negative list). That is my reading of "Klein-style"; the repo defines no caption format, so Deru can correct it. Every caption carries its own style clause so it can be pasted alone.
- **Canon source** names the vault note(s) the brief rests on. **Guesses** lists what the caption adds that canon does not say. A guess is a drawing choice, not a ruling.
- Per the image doctrine, render each iteration from scratch (rewrite the prompt, do not edit the last render), and keep the exact prompt beside every image.
- Shared constraint from canon ("Render Notes", `Zyphos Biosphere Examples`): light is scarce. Colour comes in brief flashes when sun returns; eclipse guide-lights are cold and dim, not fantasy lanterns.
- **Shared style clause (guess):** "painterly naturalist concept art, muted palette under a dim red-orange sun, restrained colour, soft atmospheric depth, no text." The vault sets no art direction; this is mine.

## Canon gaps hit

1. **Sa'ueia bodies are undefined.** `Species/Sa'ueia` gives size of group and movement ("wolves offer a loose comparison") and nothing on anatomy. Briefs 11 and 12 therefore show Sa'ueia only as figures at a distance or by their traces; any render that shows a body is inventing one.
2. **No art direction.** Palette, medium, rendering tone: not in the vault (the brand guide is for GameCult sites, not the setting).
3. **Imperial architecture has no visible form.** `Imperial Biopunk Infrastructure` lists organs, grafts, memory nodules, courts, immune checkpoints, but no built landscape; no imperial brief is offered for that reason. Same for imperial Airawa bodies (`Species/Airawa` open question: "How visible is imperial bioengineering in ordinary bodies?").
4. **Colour of Zyphos's sun and sky.** Canon says dim M-type star, ~0.006 solar luminosities; it gives no sky colour, vegetation colour or atmosphere. I used a red-orange sun and kept foliage unsaid except where canon names colours.
5. **Umbros's surface** is unspecified (`Zyphos Umbros Binary System` asks whether it hosts life). Brief 1 leaves it as a lit disc with no features named.
6. **Airawa scale, skin colour, and face** are unspecified (only scales, plates, slit nose/ears, bioluminescent plate seams).
7. **Names.** All inhabitant names are drafts (`notes/inhabitant-names-draft.md`); no caption uses one.
8. The nature of a "mother tree" (species, height, shape) is unspecified; `Umbros-Facing Lantern Trees` has no body description beyond bioluminescent canopy knots.

## Briefs

### 1. The twin in the sky (place)

- **Canon source:** `World/Zyphos Umbros Binary System` (Umbros ~10° across at 10 Earth radii, fixed in the sky; central eclipse ~1 hour; dim star).
- **Guesses:** landscape of low rolling marsh; the sun a small red-orange disc; Umbros's banding and the hue of its edge glow.
- **Caption:** A wide landscape of low wet grassland on a twilit world, with a vast pale planet hanging motionless in the sky and filling about ten degrees of it, twenty times the width of Earth's Moon, its disc dimly lit and its edge ringed by a thin glow where a small red-orange sun slides behind it in the middle of an eclipse. A shallow river reflects the dark disc and the narrow glow. The land below sits in cold blue half-shadow, with a few tiny flares of colour along the water. Low horizon, enormous sky, no figures. Painterly naturalist concept art, muted palette under a dim red-orange sun, restrained colour, soft atmospheric depth, no text.

### 2. Prismwake mats at the end of an eclipse (place / organism)

- **Guesses:** reed and pool layout; "warning blooms" drawn as small dark-rose clusters.
- **Caption:** Shallow marsh pools at the moment sunlight returns after an eclipse, thin translucent sheets of living mat spreading across the water and rippling with brief flashes of pale green, blue and rose, each flash travelling outward from a spot where a four-legged grazer has just stepped away. Near the front, a patch of the mat has gone dark and bitter-looking with small rose warning blooms where something tore at it. The rest of the marsh is dim and still, lit by the first low red-orange light across the water. Close three-quarter view from just above the water, shallow depth of field. Painterly naturalist concept art, muted palette under a dim red-orange sun, restrained colour, soft atmospheric depth, no text.
