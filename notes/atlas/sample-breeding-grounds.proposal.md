## Elaborations (each extends one canon sentence)

1. **What a visitor sees on arrival.** Extends: "Most adults visit, then leave again with their family units." Proposal: arrival is marked at the edge of the ground rather than at a gate, since no wall surrounds it; the family unit stops and waits for a caretaker to come out. **Guess:** the custom of waiting is mine. Changes if accepted: adds a courtesy, no new institution.
2. **The ground has no tallest thing.** Extends: "These sites support a mobile civilization; they are not cities." Proposal: no structure on the ground is built higher or placed more centrally than the rest, which is how a visitor can tell it from a settlement. **Guess**, supported by the note's warning that a capital breaks the system.
3. **Fungal road in.** Extends: the note's "allied organisms". Proposal: a candle fungal road (`Biosphere/Decomposers and Recyclers/Candle Fungal Roads`, which lists breeding grounds among its endpoints) leads in. That link is already canon in the fungal road note; only its use in this picture is proposed.
