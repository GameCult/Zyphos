# Metal sequestration: Meta's proposal, largely confirmed by Deru

Status: **Meta's proposal, pending Deru. Deru's answers of 2026-11-07 (15:16Z, 15:22Z, 15:35Z) confirm that matriarchs hold metal and that the Empire sought it; the guard-and-gift economy and the feudal order are still unconfirmed (see below).** Raised by Meta (with Deru in the room) in #zyphos on 2026-11-07 and written down here by Weksa so it is not lost. No vault note has been changed. Deru decides what, if anything, enters the vault.

## The proposal

- Airawa lack easy access to metal because Zyphos's biosphere sequesters it.
- Matriarch trees sequester most of it. They grow to skyscraper scale, and sit at the top of a feudal hierarchy.
- The metal sits mostly in the heartwood-equivalent of a matriarch, with traces in bark and resin.
- Matriarchs guard it and spend it. Every bit is accounted for, and is handed out as gifts.
- The empire felled matriarch trees.

## What the vault says today

The vault has no statement about metal, ore or smelting, about the height of any tree, or about a feudal order. Relevant existing text:

- Matriarch trees store communal memory, gestate Airawa young and coordinate local ecology; tribes owed them nutrients, tending, defence and deference (`Eusocial Interbeing/Species/Matriarch Trees.md:3`, `:9`, `:15`).
- Pre-imperial authority was local and regional, built around each tree (`Eusocial Interbeing/Civilizations/Pre-Imperial Airawa Tribes.md:3`).
- The Biological Memetic Engine turned the tree network into infrastructure for imperial transmission rather than removing it (`Matriarch Trees.md:21`; `Eusocial Interbeing/Ecology/Mother Tree Networks.md:7`).
- Imperial infrastructure is living machinery: organs, trees, grafts, memory nodules (`Eusocial Interbeing/Technologies/Imperial Biopunk Infrastructure.md:3`).
- The biosphere runs on less energy than Earth's (`Eusocial Interbeing/World/Zyphos Umbros Binary System.md:21`, `:53`), and the vault's rule is that physics changes the worldbuilding, not the reverse (`Eusocial Interbeing/World/Zyphos Simulation Brief.md:20`).
- Airawa tools are made with small precise lower hands (`Eusocial Interbeing/Species/Airawa.md:15`); crafts use plate inlays, resins, fungal threads and shells, not metal (`Airawa.md:33`).

## How it fits

- It gives matriarchs a reason to hold power beyond memory and birth, and it pairs with the contract-and-gift economy the vault already uses.
- It explains why Airawa technology reads as biopunk (`Imperial Biopunk Infrastructure.md:3`) and not as a metal-age one.
- Felling a matriarch would then release a stored resource and break a memory archive and a gestator together. That makes it a heavy act, and it gives the empire a motive other than control.

## Points of friction to check

- The empire's engine uses the tree network as infrastructure (`Matriarch Trees.md:21`). Felling matriarchs sits oddly with that unless it targeted hostile or disconnected trees.
- "Feudal" sits beside the vault's picture of local, negotiated, multi-species authority (`Pre-Imperial Airawa Tribes.md:3`, `Nation Scale Interbeings.md`). It is unclear whether feudal means tribes under trees, trees under a top tree, or both.
- Skyscraper scale on a low-energy world needs a check against the energy budget (`Binary System.md:53`).
