# Counterroot Embassies

Some alliances between [[Matriarch Trees]] are lodged inside the allies' bodies. A counterroot embassy is a foreign-sponsored brood of memory-bearing borers that one Matriarch deliberately admits into the outer archive roots of another. The name is a descriptive translation pending Weksa; Airawa contract dress usually identifies the specific brood, sponsor, host, and road witnesses instead of treating every compact as one institution.

## The Eaten Office

Counterroot borers feed on damaged cambium, infected root sheaths, failed graft tissue, and weakly bound archive layers. Their feeding removes pockets where [[Biosphere/Parasites and Symbionts/Mirror Amoebae|Mirror Amoebae]] and other copying organisms can make false familiarity look like healthy continuity. A well-kept brood is therefore an immune gardener as well as a parasite.

It is also an auditor with an appetite. Memory residues survive in the brood's gut symbionts and in the resin-cased waste it pushes toward nearby [[Biosphere/Decomposers and Recyclers/Candle Fungal Roads|Candle Fungal Roads]]. The record is incomplete, chewed, and biased toward whatever the host exposed to pruning, but it reveals which roots were sick, which archive layers were sacrificed, and whether the host has concealed a spreading failure.

The brood cannot remain a purely local organ. Its reproductive bodies mature only after combining sugars and memory tissue from the host with periodic resin from the sponsoring Matriarch, usually carried by contracted [[Biosphere/Couriers and Pollinators/Threadwing Couriers|Threadwing Couriers]]. Host, sponsor, brood, road, and courier route are therefore all parties to the same living office. No one of them can reproduce the arrangement alone.

## Alliance by Mutual Infestation

Rival Matriarchs usually exchange counterroot broods rather than installing one in only one direction. Each tree accepts a foreign-aligned constituency that keeps part of its immune boundary healthy, reports on its decline, and costs sugar and archive tissue to maintain. The compact makes betrayal expensive before anyone asks whether the trees trust each other.

The host can starve the embassy, divert it into worthless tissue, or burn it out. The sponsor can withhold reproductive resin, corrupt renewal stock, or redirect its courier route. The candle road can refuse to carry the brood's castings, leaving its evidence trapped inside the host's account. The brood itself can stop pruning, overfeed, conceal a rot pocket, or seek resin from a third Matriarch. Counterroot broods are colonies with hunger, memory, and descendants; calling one property does not make it obedient.

This gives Matriarch politics constituencies inside the rival's body. Airawa healers depend on the brood to keep gestation roots safe. Courier flocks depend on resin traffic and roost rights. Fungal roads gain nutrient-rich waste and privileged access to health evidence. Communities whose ancestry sits in faint outer cambium have the opposite interest: an archive layer can be declared diseased, fed to the embassy, and returned as an undetailed report that something had to be removed.

The most feared abuse is false rot. A Matriarch or Airawa keeper induces local immune distress around an inconvenient memory, then presents the embassy's consumption as independent proof that the layer was dangerous. The foreign brood did eat sick tissue. The fraud lies in who made it sick. Because the witnessing organism is genuinely foreign and performed a real repair, false rot can survive ordinary challenges under [[Ecology/Mother Tree Networks|Mother Tree Network]] law.

## Food-Web Sanctions

Breaking a counterroot compact rarely stays between two trees. A host that kills its embassy may immediately lose pruning at its gestation roots, then lose courier testimony when the sponsor closes resin traffic, then lose fungal mineral return when the road rejects contaminated castings. Pollination, safe nesting, corpse recovery, and defensive warning can peel away in different directions as each species decides whether the killing was defense, concealment, or an attempt to escape a legitimate debt.

This is why a physically stronger Matriarch may tolerate an insulting brood. Extermination can turn a manageable parasite into a coalition of unpaid witnesses and leave the victor's own food web unable to agree that it won.

The [[Technologies/Biological Memetic Engine|Biological Memetic Engine]] treats counterroot embassies as both prize and hazard. Their access to immune wounds offers an ingress route, while their divided dependence can carry evidence outward before a host network is fully overwritten. Imperial policy alternates between engineering compliant broods and declaring all foreign-root offices pathological. Native Matriarchs hear both positions as an admission that the empire has not found a clean way to monopolize them.

## Open Pressures

- Can a counterroot brood defect from both sponsor and host, and which nation-scale body may recognize it without treating living diplomats as stolen tissue?
- Who can prove that a consumed archive layer was made sick on purpose when the embassy's evidence begins only after feeding?
- What happens when a sponsor Matriarch dies but its broods remain indispensable inside several rival trees and their reproductive resin is running out?
