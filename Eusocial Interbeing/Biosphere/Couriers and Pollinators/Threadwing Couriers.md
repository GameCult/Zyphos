# Threadwing Couriers

Threadwing couriers are flying and gliding animals that move living cargo and information between groves, wetlands, reefs, and breeding grounds. They carry pollen analogues, wound scents, gut symbionts, memories of routes, and grievances over how others have been treated. Other organisms depend on them for pollination, scouting, messages, and warnings.

Ribbonlike sensory vanes take the place of feathers. Calling threadwings birds is a useful first approximation, but their senses and obligations need an explanation of their own.

## Bodies That Sense and Remember

Each vane detects pressure, chemical gradients, static charge, thermal plumes, and memory residues. The ribbons naturally fray and regrow, leaving fibers in nests and courier stations. [[Biosphere/Decomposers and Recyclers/Lattice Ants|Lattice Ants]] can read those fibers. Sa'auei'a travelers can braid them into warning knots, though the knots do not last long.

Threadwings remember through several channels: gut symbionts, residues on their vanes, route songs, nesting fibers, and lantern pulses. Remembered insults also influence their behavior.

Each animal has its own preferences, pair bonds, rivalries, and grudges. Its gut symbionts—the organisms living within its digestive system—and its nesting routes also carry obligations involving whole colonies. These demands can differ from the animal's immediate wishes. A threadwing may want berries while its microbiome wants a treaty enforced. Both matter to what it does.

## Work, Payment, and Refusal

Threadwings provide several distinct services:

- They pollinate [[Biosphere/Primary Producers/Umbros-Facing Lantern Trees|Umbros-Facing Lantern Trees]].
- They warn [[Biosphere/Primary Producers/Prismwake Mats|Prismwake Mats]] about destructive grazers.
- They carry reef mineral tastes inland.
- They bring reports of sickness from [[Biosphere/Decomposers and Recyclers/Candle Fungal Roads|Candle Fungal Roads]].

Their partners pay with nectar, warm roosts, salt, parasite removal through preening, and dependable light along their routes. Clean roosts, nectar delivered on time, respectful distance from nests, parasite removal, and accurate route signals encourage cooperation.

A partner can lose that cooperation by stealing eggs, poisoning nectar, sending false lantern pulses, capturing threadwings by force, or trying to edit their gut memory without negotiation. Threadwings can withhold pollination or scramble the order of messages. They can also carry a grievance to someone with teeth. Delivery is part of their power; so is deciding what gets delivered, when, and to whom.

## Priorities and Threat Responses

A threadwing needs to feed, mate, protect its nest, and keep its symbiont cargo alive and viable. It also works to maintain its reputation along its routes. Its choices therefore depend on more than hunger or immediate danger: the nest, cargo, mate, and history of dealings along a route all matter.

When threatened, threadwings may fall silent as a flock, provide false routes, produce alarm veils, deliver grievances, dump parasites, or coordinate attacks on exposed tissue. These responses include warning, deception, disclosure, and direct physical attack; the particular response matters when interpreting what a flock is doing.

## Portraying a Threadwing

A threadwing NPC is a courier with a stake in both the message and the road. It has enough ownership of each to hold opinions about their use. Small things can distract it, while its larger political choices remain precise.

Track its hunger, trust in a route, risk to its nest, cargo integrity, bond with its mate, and the intensity of its grudges. These give its decisions a basis beyond whatever message someone wants it to carry.

Make those decisions visible through vane flutter, circling a route, refusing to land, shedding fibers, dropping something on a specific target, or mimicking a nest call. A threadwing that lands only on one faction's marker is also making a readable choice.
