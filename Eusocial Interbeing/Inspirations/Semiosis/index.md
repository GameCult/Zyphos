# Semiosis Inspirations

Sue Burke’s *Semiosis* offers a way to think about plants as intelligent actors whose decisions can determine what a society is able to do. Coexistence requires negotiation with nonhuman life; intelligence does not need a humanoid body to hold that much power.

For this vault, the useful inspiration is how species negotiate their ecological relationships and how nonhuman beings pursue strategies of their own. These are questions to develop in this setting, not a template to copy from *Semiosis*.
