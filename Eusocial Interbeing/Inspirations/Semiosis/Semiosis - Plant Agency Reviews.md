# Semiosis - Plant Agency Reviews

Public reviews of Sue Burke’s *Semiosis* repeatedly describe colonists learning that intelligent plants and the planet’s ecology can act on their own interests. The colonists must deal with living things that can affect what happens to them, rather than treating the planet as a passive backdrop.

This is useful inspiration for [[Ecological Bargaining]] and [[Ecological Memory Partners]]: relationships in which nonhuman organisms remember how they have been treated and can change their cooperation over time.

## Translation Into This Setting

Plants should have interests and responses of their own. Casting them only as wise elders, or as networks that decorate the setting without affecting events, would lose what makes this reference useful.

A mother tree could nurture others, pursue a strategy, coerce them, or act from fear. Organisms that remember past relationships could reward generosity without being morally pure. Their capacity to remember and cooperate need not make them benevolent.

These are design possibilities drawn from the reviews, not adopted *Semiosis* canon.
