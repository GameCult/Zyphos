## Useful Pressure

Avatar presents a living network through which ecology supports communication, sacred relationships, and continuity across generations. It offers an inspiration for this setting: organisms and their connections can carry a society's memory.

## Re-engineering

This setting's nearest equivalent is [[Mother Tree Networks]]. They can preserve communal memory, but access to that memory and ancestral continuity can also depend on obligations. A society's dependence on the network can become a hostage relationship.

The departure from Avatar is an emphasis on who controls this dependence and what they demand in return. The design question is: what happens when the archive wants payment, and one society invents a tool that lets it stop paying?
