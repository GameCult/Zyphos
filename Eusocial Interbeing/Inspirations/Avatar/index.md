# Avatar Inspirations

Avatar provides a loose starting point for this setting: a living network and a dominant humanoid species whose life is closely tied to its ecology. This vault rebuilds that premise around relationships between eusocial species, differences between continents, and ecological systems that societies depend on—and can use to coerce one another.
