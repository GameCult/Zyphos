# Avatar - Na'vi Position Rebuilt

The [[Airawa]] borrow a broad premise from Avatar’s Na’vi: a dominant intelligent species whose way of life is closely tied to local ecology. That relationship is the inspiration; the Airawa’s history and place in the world differ.

Ecological dependence gave others power over Airawa communities before any empire existed. Mother trees preserved communal memory and could hold it hostage. The tribe that later became the Airawa Empire invented a biological memetic engine to escape that coercion. The invention then became a means of imperial control: it let the tribe overwrite rival tribes through the living networks those rivals trusted.

The Airawa also do not provide a universal model for intelligent life in this setting. They never evolved on the continent where the [[Sa'ueia]] developed as another dominant intelligent species. The setting keeps the interest of a people bound to living ecology while allowing those relationships—and the societies built through them—to differ across the world.
