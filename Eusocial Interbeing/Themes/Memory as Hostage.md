# Memory as Hostage

Whoever preserves a community's memory can gain power over the people who need it. Airawa communities depend on [[Matriarch Trees]] to preserve knowledge beyond living memory. The trees demand care and service in return. Communities that fail or defy them risk losing access to ancestral memory, ecological warnings, and the remembered basis of their laws.

The [[Biological Memetic Engine]] was created to free the Airawa from this dependency. It then turned the tree network into a means of transmitting imperial influence. Memory remained a tool of coercion, now under imperial control.

For the [[Sa'ueia]], memory is spread across mobile families, breeding grounds, ecological partners, and obligations renewed through repeated travel. This makes it harder to seize: no single keeper holds it all. But dispersion, disaster, or broken trust can sever the relationships that keep it available, making that memory easier to lose.
