# Sa'auei'a Mobile Technologies

Sa'auei'a technology is advanced equipment designed to travel and be repaired along the way. It also helps its users maintain relationships with other species. They move through ecologies whose inhabitants remember how they have been treated, so returning to a place means returning to those relationships.

## Likely Forms

These technologies may include:

- Collapsible shelters grown from dormant symbionts.
- Route-memory instruments that record ecological obligations as well as routes.
- Medical grafts maintained by breeding-ground specialists.
- Languages of scent, vibration, and pattern for communicating with partner species.
- Portable archives distributed across families and living companions.

The [[Airawa Empire]] expands its power by capturing networks. Sa'auei'a technologies support a different way of extending influence: returning repeatedly and building trust with those they encounter.
