# Imperial Biopunk Infrastructure

Imperial biopunk infrastructure is the living machinery the empire uses to govern. It grows from the [[Biological Memetic Engine]]: organs, trees, grafts, controlled parasites, bodies tuned to their castes, memory nodules, courts that use pheromonal signals, and immune checkpoints all serve imperial administration.

These systems help administrators reach consensus, watch borders, control who is licensed to reproduce, and coordinate military activity. They also let the empire treat ideological conformity as a public-health concern and revise history through living archives. Biology carries both the services people depend on and the controls imposed on them.

## Limits

This infrastructure works best where living networks are connected, the empire can read their signals, and imperial controls already govern what crosses their boundaries. Its reach depends on those conditions.

It is weaker against native [[Nation Scale Interbeings]], [[Disconnected Tree Networks]], and the [[Sa'ueia Continent]]. In these systems, cooperation runs through many local ecological relationships rather than one continental archive. The empire cannot control them as reliably through the machinery it uses at home.
