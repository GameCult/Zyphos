# Imperial Discovery of the Sa'ueia

The [[Airawa Empire]] discovers an advanced civilization on the [[Sa'ueia Continent]] whose way of life challenges imperial doctrine. The Sa'ueia have developed without mother trees, maintain social discipline without imperial memetics, and sustain a lasting political order without fixed capitals. Knowledge, law, craft, and diplomacy travel with their family units.

Their existence gives the empire a problem: a civilization can endure without the arrangements its founding story presents as necessary. Imperial society is one possible way to live, rather than the inevitable result of natural law.

## Imperial Reaction

The ruling class launches the [[Xenophobic Memetic Campaign]] to prevent that implication from circulating. It portrays Sa'ueia life as contamination, animal mimicry, a reproductive threat, and ecological sabotage.

These claims reach imperial citizens through more than public messages. The empire uses living networks to shape belief through memetic organisms, engineered dreams, sensory associations, and inherited compliance cues. Its campaign against the Sa'ueia is propaganda grown into bodies.
