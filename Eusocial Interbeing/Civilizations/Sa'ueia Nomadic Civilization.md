# Sa'ueia Nomadic Civilization

Sa'ueia civilization is highly advanced, but its knowledge and institutions do not depend on settling permanently as the empire does. Mobile families preserve and pass on knowledge through ritual routes, relationships with ecological partners, and crafts they can carry with them. Sacred communal breeding sites provide lasting places of return.

## Institutions

[[Sa'ueia Family Units]] carry law, skills, collective memory, and diplomacy as they travel. When several families converge, route assemblies provide a place to resolve disputes.

[[Breeding Grounds]] are sacred reproductive commons. They anchor care for infants and infirm adults, reproductive rituals, and the recognition of legitimacy between families.

Ecological partners also shape how this civilization works: they remember generosity and punish extraction. Maintaining those relationships matters to a mobile society as much as preserving its crafts and laws.

## Misread by the Empire

The [[Airawa Empire]] sees Sa'ueia mobility as evidence of primitiveness. Its own civilization depends so heavily on controlling networks and fixed biological infrastructure that it struggles to recognize advancement organized in another way.

Imperial arrogance contributes to this judgment, but the problem runs deeper. The empire's past choices have narrowed what it can recognize as civilization.
